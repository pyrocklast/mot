
# mot launcher

a launcher, with a **m**inimum **o**f **t**hings.

Your apps as words on your wallpaper, with the time and the date. No icons, no widgets, no internet, nothing collected.

It's for Android 15 and up, in English and French.

## What it does

- Favorites adapt to the space on your home screen, including when the clock or date is hidden. No scrolling, compressed rows or overflow shortcut; manage any saved favorites that no longer fit in Settings. Long press and drag to reorder.
- Swipe up for all your apps, down for notifications, left and right for two apps you pick. Settings: long press the home screen, or the gear in the app drawer.
- Tap the time or the date to open your clock or calendar, or any app you choose.
- Search ignores accents and case, puts the best matches first, and can optionally open the only match after two letters (off by default).
- Long press an app to rename it, hide it or uninstall it. Work apps get a small dot, and Private Space has its own list.
- The clock and date have one coherent style and follow your system language, 12/24-hour setting and font scaling. Show or hide each; adjust only the shared app-name size.
- The text colour matches your wallpaper. Settings follow your phone's theme.

## Install

Add `https://github.com/pyrocklast/mot` to [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), or get the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/pyrocklast/mot/releases). Then set mot launcher as your Home app.

## Permissions

| Permission | What for |
|---|---|
| Expand status bar | Opening notifications |
| Request delete packages | Uninstalling apps |
| Set alarm | Opening your alarms |
| Access hidden profiles | Showing Private Space |
